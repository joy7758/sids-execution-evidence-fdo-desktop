# Submission Checklist for Author

- Confirm the final title exactly matches the portal title and PDF title.
- Confirm the portal abstract matches the latest submission-facing abstract and contains no citations.
- Confirm author names, affiliations, and contact details in the submission system.
- Confirm any funding statement or acknowledgment text required by the venue.
- Confirm conflict-of-interest declarations in the portal.
- Confirm anonymization status in the PDF, source package, and any auxiliary files.
- Confirm the final PDF page count under the actual venue template.
- Confirm figure readability at final two-column scale.
- Confirm bibliography rendering and citation order in the final PDF.
- Confirm any artifact statement wording does not expose external release details unless the venue explicitly asks for them.
- Confirm whether the venue allows appendices, supplementary material, or separate artifact notes.
- Confirm that all uploaded files are the intended final versions and not staging intermediates.
